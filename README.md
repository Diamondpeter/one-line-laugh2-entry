One funny line for the make.fun bounty [The One-Line Laugh](https://make.fun/bounty/u-ronigrv-the-one-line-laugh-7ccca46f): a single sentence of at most 200 characters, judged head-to-head by a house judge on surprise, everyday truth and economy of words.

The entry:

> The Wi-Fi only drops when I'm about to win — my router has a gambling problem.

`main.py` writes the line to `/output/entry.txt`.
